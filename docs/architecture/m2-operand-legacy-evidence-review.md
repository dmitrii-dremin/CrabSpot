# Legacy replay inheritance: measurement limitation

The current operand legacy replays may be retained as inherited source-linked regression evidence after terminal success. The absence of an executable hash captured before the earliest family does not by itself require repeating identical successful executions. This decision does not convert a later hash or filesystem timestamp into a retrospective measurement.

The coordinator reports an immediately preceding all-release-bin/example build from the same frozen sources. The per-family runner records unchanged crates/tools hashes, commands, terminal results and logs; subsequent Cargo messages indicate Finished without compilation. I inspected the current evidence through the six completed application, monitor, mirror, String, String-body and String-equality families. The older six tools do not independently record executable hashes. The Throwable tool does record its executable hashes before and after its actual replay. It had not completed when this decision was written.

Required inheritance conditions: retain the preceding exact-source build evidence; identify Cargo manifests/lock, compiler and build configuration; preserve source/fixture/helper/platform identities relevant to each family; document cache ownership and absence of another writer or intervening rebuild; capture existing executable hashes and timestamps now with their actual measurement time; compare those bytes with the final executables. Do not claim that timestamps prove earlier contents or that the six earlier launches had pre/post executable measurement. Preserve each earlier log/target and terminal outcome rather than presenting these as fresh final-gate executions.

Any unexplained input or executable difference, intervening compilation/configuration change, or conflicting cache writer requires a rerun of affected families under full pre/post pins. Adding an independent new operand example, new operand-only tooling/reference metadata or CI/documentation does not require repeating old families if their transitive execution inputs remain unchanged. Fresh all-target lint, workspace/release tests and the new operand matrices remain separate requirements. A nonterminal or failed family cannot count as success.

This is an explicit evidence-strength judgment: the inherited six are source-linked regression evidence with a missing pre-run binary measurement, not a cryptographic attestation of every earlier process image. No actual change or substitution is indicated by the current record. Final combined acceptance remains pending terminal results and the coordinator's final inventory audit.
